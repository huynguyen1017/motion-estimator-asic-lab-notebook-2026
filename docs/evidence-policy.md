# Evidence provenance

The three screenshots were supplied by Huy Nguyen during the guided laboratory session. They are preserved as received, without generated replacement graphics. They show two VPN states and a local xterm; none shows a remote waveform or physical implementation.

The two terminal transcripts were supplied as pasted text. They are transcribed records, not automatically captured raw logs. The student identifier is replaced by `<student-id>`, pasted email-link markup is converted to plain command text, and presentation whitespace is normalized. The recorded results and warning messages are retained. No password or authentication code is stored.

The manifest contains SHA-256 hashes of the public evidence files for reproducibility. Hashes establish file identity, not independent verification of events. Screenshot filenames identify their sequence, not independently verified capture timestamps.

Only student-supplied execution evidence is included in this milestone. Separate clock-expression experiments, prepared slides and other candidate implementations are not represented as student-executed motion-estimator results.

The source requirements are the supplied ENGR 852 course-project rubric, pages 1–2, and the course remote-login tutorials. They are referenced by title rather than redistributed. No claim of university endorsement or completed ASIC implementation is made.
